📊 Statistical Analysis, Seasonality & Hydro-Meteorological Coupling (2023–2026)

NASA FLDAS: Root-Zone Soil Moisture

Cyclical Behavior: Peaks annually during the Southwest Monsoon (~0.36 m³/m³) and troughs every Q1 dry season (~0.14 m³/m³), defining our critical drought threshold (< 0.16 m³/m³).

NASA FLDAS: Daily Rainfall Flux

Precipitation Pulse: High-frequency convective spikes drive soil recharge. Prolonged suppression during El Niño triggers rapid root-zone depletion.

NASA FLDAS: Surface Air Temperature

Thermal Harmonic: Follows a strict annual solar harmonic between ~288 K (15°C) and ~302 K (29°C) across the broader SEA domain.
